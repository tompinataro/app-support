My Inventory by Pinataro — Privacy

Effective November 9, 2026. This policy describes the native iOS and Android app published by Thomas Anthony Pinataro.

Data on your device

Room names, belongings, valuations, notes and photographs are stored locally on your device. The app does not connect to an insurer or automatically upload your inventory.

The native package does not include an advertising or analytics SDK. There is no developer-operated sign-in service for this app.

Sharing, links and external content

When you choose to export or share a file, the destination you select receives that file. Review it before sharing. External links, and any remote images or reference content displayed by the app, make requests to the relevant website. Those providers receive network information such as an IP address and request headers; their own privacy policies apply.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it using the app or device controls. Removing the app does not remove backup files or copies you exported elsewhere. Device backups are governed by your operating-system settings. You control the retention and deletion of exported copies.

Support

If you email support, your email address and the information you include are received in the support mailbox. Avoid including sensitive records or recordings unless needed for your request. To ask about access to or deletion of support correspondence, contact TixPy@pinataro.com.

Website hosting

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Requests to this website are subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.
